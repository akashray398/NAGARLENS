## 1. Asset Category Condition Indicators

- **Catch Basins**: Invert silt depth (2D bounding box area ratio), grate damage, solid waste obstruction.
- **Storm Water Drains**: Cross-sectional flow constriction, vegetation intrusion, masonry lining cracking.
- **Manholes**: Frame displacement, missing lid hazard, surcharge overflow stains.
- **Culverts**: Inlet debris damming, barrel sediment deposition, outlet scour.
