## 1. Operational Limitations & Disclaimers

1. **Decision Support Signal**: Predictive risk scores are decision-support indicators, NOT official structural engineering certifications.
2. **2D Photographic Coverage**: Area severity estimates are derived from 2D photographic perspective bounding boxes; hydraulic capacity calculations require physical gauge sensors.
3. **Human Review Requirement**: High-impact maintenance dispatch actions require verification by qualified municipal engineering personnel.
