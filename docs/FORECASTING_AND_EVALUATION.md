## 1. Evaluation Metrics

- **Precision & Recall**: Evaluated against confirmed human reviewer feedback labels (`CONFIRMED`, `REJECTED_FALSE_POSITIVE`).
- **Mean Absolute Error (MAE)**: Measures discrepancy between predicted severity score and reviewer-adjusted severity score.
